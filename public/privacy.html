Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

session ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth). During normal use we store your workout templates, session logs, personal records, body metrics you choose to enter, and any feedback you submit. We do not collect payment information.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the app — syncing your workouts across sessions, computing personal records and progress charts, and generating AI-assisted training plans. We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

We use Supabase for database and authentication hosting, and Anthropic's Claude API to power AI plan generation. Workout prompts sent to Claude contain only anonymized training preferences (days per week, focus areas) — no personal identifiers.

Data retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time via Settings → Delete Account.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Database access is protected by row-level security policies so users can only read and write their own records.
